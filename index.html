THE COMPLETE SYSTEM

Seven stages. One successful update.

The threshold circuit decides when enough energy has accumulated; it does not regulate 3.3 V. Regulation happens only after the high-side switch enables the buck-boost converter.

Smartphone→NFC field→Passive antenna→ST25 VEH→4700 µF VSTORE→Threshold + switch→3.3 V + load
Current laboratory observationThe 4700 µF storage capacitor charges in approximately 10–15 seconds in the present setup. This remains preliminary until distance, alignment, endpoints and repeat count are recorded.
