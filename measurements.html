ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION

01

Automatic switching occurred

Version 1 proved that a discrete high-side path could connect the load without a manual switch.

02

The transition was not sharp enough

Q1 pulled VGATE close to ground early; the observed load transition therefore depended strongly on the IRF9530N gate-source characteristic.

03

Version 2 is justified

A comparator or improved threshold network is needed to create a controlled turn-on point and hysteresis suitable for the stored-energy budget.
